Learn Dutch from the websites you already use. DutchMate helps English- and Telugu-speaking learners understand Dutch in context, keep useful vocabulary, and practise it in short local learning flows.

WHAT'S NEW IN v0.6.0

- Practical Dutch now includes a reviewed supermarket topic with A1 and A2 lessons for useful everyday situations.
- A compact focused popup makes Read, Notice, Practise, and Keep easier to follow in a small browser window.
- Browser updates preserve your local learning history, including saved items, lesson progress, and practice evidence.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Understand Dutch
1.1 Select a Dutch word, phrase, or sentence on a webpage.
1.2 View English and Telugu translations while keeping the page in view.

2. Save useful vocabulary
2.1 Save selected Dutch words you want to remember.
2.2 Find and manage them later in the Saved section.

3. Review what you learned
3.1 Open Today to complete a short daily review.
3.2 Revisit vocabulary and learning items that need more practice.

4. Learn practical Dutch
4.1 Open Lessons and choose an everyday topic.
4.2 Work through reviewed examples and exercises at the available level.

5. Practise verbs and Dutch tenses
5.1 Open a Verb Journey or Dutch tense practice activity.
5.2 Compare forms, learn them in context, and complete the exercises.

PRIVACY & DATA

Your saved vocabulary, learning progress, and settings are stored locally in your browser. Selected text may be sent to the configured translation service to generate translations. Some translation features require an internet connection. DutchMate does not require an account or cloud sync for its learning features.
